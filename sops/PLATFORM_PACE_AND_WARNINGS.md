# SOP: Platform pace and warnings (redacted sample)

**Principle:** on any third-party platform the system works the way a careful person would: spread through the day, one session at a time, within the platform's rules. A warning is a hard stop, not a speed bump.

## Pace
1. Work is spread across the day in short, normal-sized sessions. No bursts, no "catch-up sprints".
2. One browser session and one tab per agent; a cap on concurrent heavy sessions across the system.
3. Searches are short and spaced out. A thin result set ends the session; it never triggers more searching.
4. Every one-to-one message still needs the CEO's yes (see DECISION_RIGHTS).
5. Quality over volume: an item without a source link and the verbatim text is not counted.

## Warnings → STOP
Any checkpoint, captcha, restriction or limit notice, forced logout, or unusual-activity message:
1. **Stop** all activity on that platform, for every agent, in the same minute.
2. Save a screenshot and open a priority-1 board row.
3. The orchestrator tells the CEO **within 15 minutes**.
4. Resume **only** after the CEO reviews and says yes. A short postmortem follows.

Machine problems (low memory, a crashed tab) use a separate code and are never logged as a platform event.
