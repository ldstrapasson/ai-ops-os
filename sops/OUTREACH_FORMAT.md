# SOP: Outreach format (redacted sample)

Every outbound message has three parts, in this order, and is sent only after the CEO's yes.

| Part | What it says |
|---|---|
| **The problem** | The specific operational problem the person described or showed, in their terms |
| **The fix** | One concrete change that would help, in plain words |
| **The offer** | One call to action: a short diagnostic call |

## Rules
- Direct message: three short lines, one per part. Email: bold labels, about 90–130 words, no headings.
- One call to action. No prices, no guarantees, no invented proof (quotes must be verbatim).
- Email goes only to an address the person published themselves; never generic inboxes, never a guessed address.
- Check the recipient on screen before sending; each message carries an idempotency key so it is never sent twice.

## Example (fictional)
> **The problem:** You mentioned month-end close takes your team two weeks.
> **The fix:** One owner per close step and a shared checklist usually cuts that in half.
> **The offer:** Happy to map it with you in a 30-minute diagnostic. Want the link?

*Fictional example: not a real person or company.*
