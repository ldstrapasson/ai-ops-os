# Agent roster

11 specialists + 1 orchestrator. Each has a CANON (best-practice reference), a WORKFLOW card (end-to-end steps) and a lessons log. Every one-to-one message that goes out under my name is approved by me first.

| # | Agent | Owns | Explicitly does NOT own |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | **Orchestrator (COO)** | Priorities, single source of truth, handoffs, approval batches, daily report to CEO | Day-to-day lane work |
| 1 | **Opportunity Dig** | Searching and triaging opportunities against hard filters, plus the submit queue | Social engagement, content |
| 2 | **Social Engagement** | Following target customers' public conversations and my notifications; replies within rules I approved, at human pace; stops at the first platform warning | One-to-one outreach, scheduling |
| 3 | **LI Pipeline** | Profile-viewer triage and approved one-to-one outreach to target customers | Public replies |
| 4 | **Content Scheduler** | Post queue from approved drafts: lessons + verified proof posts | Writing new copy, outreach |
| 5 | **Email Demand** | Follow-ups and email reinforcement, only to addresses people published; drafts only, I approve | Social channels |
| 6 | **Marketing** | Content strategy; briefs the Content Scheduler | Direct posting / paid spend |
| 7 | **Sales** | Pipeline quality, conversation SLA, follow-ups, the commercial outcome | Spending outreach credits |
| 8 | **Ops Delivery** | Client implementation playbooks and diagnostic readouts (wakes when a deal or diagnostic is booked) | Prospecting |
| 9 | **Tech Build** | Agent-built software (ThreadProof), only after product-gate approval | Inventing products |
| 10 | **Market Research** | Weekly opportunity scan → top-10 demand list → proposals | Coding |
| 11 | **General Assistant** | General requests and one-off tasks outside the other lanes | Any specialist lane (hands it back to the owner) |

Headcount is frozen at 11: adding a lane needs a written cost and outcome case.
