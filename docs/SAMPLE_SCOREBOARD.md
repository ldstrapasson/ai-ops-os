# Sample daily scoreboard (illustrative, redacted)

> Format is the real one. Values below are **illustrative**, rounded and anonymised. They are not a copy of a live day.

```
2026-10-13 | COMMERCIAL: open_conversations=12 proposals=1 inmails=0 first_touch=50/50 replies=2 connect=open | PIPELINE: submits=3 queued=0 blockers=1
```

| Field | Meaning | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| open_conversations | Live commercial conversations | Sales / LI Pipeline |
| proposals | Proposals in flight that day | Sales |
| inmails | Paid InMails used (cap awareness) | LI Pipeline |
| first_touch | New first-touch comments vs 50/day target | LI Engage |
| replies | Nested/sibling replies (not counted toward target) | LI Engage |
| connect | Weekly connection limit open/paused | LI Pipeline |
| submits / queued | Opportunity submits done / still queued | Opportunity Dig |
| blockers | Human blockers open (OTP, password, approval) | Orchestrator |

**Rule:** the commercial funnel and the opportunity pipeline are never mixed into one number.
